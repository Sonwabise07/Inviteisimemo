About us

Digital invitations made for the way South Africa celebrates.

Invitisimemo brings beautiful invite design, WhatsApp-first sharing, personalised guest links, and practical RSVP tools into one simple platform for hosts and their guests.

Why we built Invitisimemo

Most invitation tools feel generic, expensive, or disconnected from how people actually organise celebrations. South African hosts often plan across family groups, WhatsApp chats, cultural traditions, and fast-changing guest lists.

Invitisimemo is built around that reality: create quickly, customise deeply, share easily, and keep RSVP information tidy without making guests download an app.

What we support

  • Birthdays, weddings, lobola, umemulo, graduations, showers, and more.
  • Free build and preview, with paid publishing when you are ready to share.
  • Guest RSVP collection with dietary notes, messages, and personalised links.
  • Host tools for email summaries, CSV exports, broadcasts, and gift registries.
Local first

Designed for South African celebrations, with room to grow into more languages and regions.

Guest friendly

Guests can open a link, enjoy the invite, and RSVP without accounts or complicated steps.

Host ready

The dashboard keeps your guest list, sharing links, RSVP data, and event updates in one place.
